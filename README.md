# 🎓 Campus Career & Placement Intelligence Platform


An AI-powered, institutional-grade career intelligence platform designed to bridge the gap between academic learning and professional success. Built for students, career counselors, and university administrators.

## 🌟 Vision

The **Campus Career Platform** transforms traditional placement cells into data-driven career hubs. By leveraging AI-powered analytics, it provides students with actionable insights for their career journeys while giving administrators a bird's-eye view of institutional placement health.

## 🚀 Key Modules

### 👨‍🎓 For Students
- **Smart Dashboard**: Personalized analytics on readiness, project progress, and skill gaps.
- **Career Toolkit**: AI-powered resume building, interview preparation, and skill tracking.
- **Wellbeing Hub**: A dedicated space for mental health, featuring guided breathing, mood tracking, and professional resources.
- **Success Stories**: Real-world case studies of students landing roles at FAANG and top-tier companies.
- **Placement Feedback**: A multi-step form to share interview experiences and institutional memory.

### 🏫 For Colleges
- **Placement Analytics**: Real-time tracking of student placements, average packages, and company performance.
- **Institutional Health**: Metrics on student readiness and academic trends.
- **Employer Relations**: Tools to manage corporate partnerships and hiring drives.
